The AcuMyo approach

Understand first. Treat with purpose.

Every treatment begins with professional assessment. The clinic's approach considers more than the area where discomfort appears, including posture, movement patterns, muscle and fascial tension, work habits and everyday physical demands.

Good to know

Questions before your first visit?

A few helpful details about appointments, treatment planning and what to expect when you contact AcuMyo.

Visit the full Q&A ↗

The clinic lists concerns such as neck, shoulder, back and lower-back pain, muscle tension, joint stiffness, headaches, postural problems, sports injuries and movement limitations. The appropriate care depends on an individual assessment.

AcuMyo's published Q&A states that most sessions are approximately 45 minutes. The duration can depend on the treatment and your individual needs.

There is no fixed number of sessions for everyone. Your practitioner can discuss a suitable plan after assessing your condition and reviewing your progress.

The clinic's published information says direct booking is usually possible without a referral. Some insurance policies or individual circumstances may require one, so check before your appointment if this applies to you.

AcuMyo states that eligible invoices, receipts or reports may be provided for reimbursement. Coverage and approval depend on your individual policy and insurer.

The clinic may consider combinations of physiotherapy, acupuncture, manual therapy, therapeutic massage, dry needling, Hijama and rehabilitation exercises, depending on the assessment and clinical suitability.
