# AcuMyo Physiotherapy Center – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

AcuMyo Physiotherapy Center is a Dubai-based healthcare and rehabilitation clinic located in Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC). The clinic provides personalized physiotherapy, rehabilitation, acupuncture, manual therapy, dry needling, therapeutic massage, and other integrative treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating AcuMyo's integrated approach to physical care, helping visitors discover relevant treatments, understand the clinic's services, and find a clear pathway to book an appointment.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Messaging and Booking Journey
- Strengthened the homepage introduction to communicate AcuMyo's physiotherapy, acupuncture, and rehabilitation services.
- Improved the presentation of the clinic's value proposition and treatment approach.
- Made appointment calls to action more prominent to help visitors move towards booking.

### 2. Better Organisation of Treatments and Services
- Restructured treatment and service information into clearer content sections.
- Improved the presentation of available therapies and clinical services.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify relevant treatment options without navigating through scattered information.

### 3. Improved Contact Information Consistency
- Addressed the discrepancy between the mobile number displayed on the website and the number associated with WhatsApp booking.
- Established a clearer and more consistent contact presentation.
- Improved the visibility of contact and appointment pathways to reduce confusion during enquiries.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a more professional healthcare-focused appearance that reflects AcuMyo's physiotherapy and rehabilitation positioning.
- **Layout:** Improved section hierarchy, spacing, alignment, and content organisation.
- **Typography:** Established clearer distinctions between headings, service descriptions, and supporting information.
- **User Experience:** Simplified treatment discovery and improved access to appointment and contact options.
- **Consistency:** Strengthened consistency across service sections, calls to action, and contact information.
- **Overall Appearance:** Developed a more cohesive homepage that communicates the clinic's services and personalized approach to patient care.
